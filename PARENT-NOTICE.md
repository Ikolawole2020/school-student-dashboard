                     STARLIGHT MODEL SCHOOL
                    A NOTE FOR PARENTS
================================================================================

                   EMPOWERING MINDS, SHAPING FUTURES.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dear Parents,

We have been improving the way our school records information, and we wanted to
share what this means for you and your child.

This note covers the changes that affect families directly.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AN ACTION WE NEED FROM YOU

  PLEASE MAKE SURE WE HAVE A CORRECT CONTACT NUMBER FOR YOUR CHILD.

  We can now record a parent's or guardian's phone number on your child's
  record. This is the single most useful thing you can do for us.

  A correct number means we can reach you quickly if your child becomes ill or
  if anything else requires a call. The school office can add it for you, and
  it can also be updated from the website if that is easier.

  It is worth checking this now, rather than when you need it.


================================================================================
 WHEN THE SCHOOL IS CLOSED
================================================================================

  The school now keeps a holiday calendar. Every public holiday and school
  closure is listed with its date and a short note explaining why.

  You will find it on the school website, under "Holidays" in the menu. It does
  not require signing in, so you can check it at any time.

  The reason for the note is simple. Previously, an error could be made where a
  whole class was marked absent on a day the school was actually shut. That
  cannot happen now, because the calendar is checked before any register is
  marked.


================================================================================
 ATTENDANCE
================================================================================

  Teachers now record attendance digitally every school day, instead of keeping
  paper registers.

  WHY THIS IS BETTER FOR YOUR CHILD

  Paper registers are easy to misplace, easy to lose, and difficult to review
  across a term. Digital records mean that a pattern of absence or lateness is
  visible to the school early, which is usually when support is most useful.

  WHY THIS MATTERS MOST FOR A HEALTHY CHILD

  Regular attendance is one of the strongest indicators of how a child is
  getting on. If you notice your child's attendance slipping, the school can
  usually do something about it sooner now.

  A PARENT VIEW IS COMING

  At present this information is held by the school. We are building a way for
  parents to see their own child's attendance and results directly, and this is
  one of the next improvements we intend to deliver.

================================================================================
 YOUR CHILD'S RESULTS
================================================================================

  A change that may directly affect your child this term.

  If a child is unwell and sits only some of the examinations, their result is
  now recorded for the subjects they actually sat. Previously a partial set of
  results could be treated as incomplete.

  Put simply: a child who missed an exam through no fault of their own is no
  longer disadvantaged for it.

  PROMOTION HAS ALSO BEEN MADE FAIRER

  A child is now moved to the next class only when every subject for the term
  has been marked. This protects children from being moved up a class on the
  strength of a single result.


================================================================================
 SCHOOL NEWS AND EVENTS
================================================================================

  The school website now carries regular news, announcements, events and
  achievements, with photographs.

  Previously the front page could only be changed by someone able to edit the
  website itself, so news was rare and often out of date. Information about
  events, competitions and achievements will now appear as they happen.

  We would encourage you to check the site regularly.


================================================================================
 USING THE WEBSITE ON A PHONE
================================================================================

  The website has been rebuilt to work properly on a phone.

  If you have been trying to check something on your phone and finding it
  awkward or difficult to tap, this should now be much easier.

  The site also follows the light or dark setting of your own phone
  automatically, so it is easier to read in bright sunlight and easier on the
  eyes at night.


================================================================================
 WHAT WE ARE WORKING ON NEXT
================================================================================

  We are continuing to develop the platform. Our current priorities are:

  -  A parent view, so you can see your child's attendance, results and school
     notices in one place

  -  Report cards you can print, showing results, position in class and the
     teacher's remarks

  -  Fee and payment records, so you can see what is owed and what has been
     paid

  We welcome your suggestions about what would be most useful to you.


================================================================================
 THANK YOU
================================================================================

  These changes were made to give the school better records, to support your
  child more quickly when they need it, and to keep you informed without you
  having to chase information.

  If you have any questions, please contact the school office. If your contact
  number is not on your child's record, this is a good moment to have it added.
