Dear Parents, Teachers and Students,

Over the past few weeks the school platform has been rebuilt and extended.

This update explains, in plain language, what has changed, why we made each

================================================================================
 1.  ATTENDANCE IS NOW RECORDED EVERY SCHOOL DAY
================================================================================

WHAT IT DOES

  Teachers can now mark a daily register directly from the platform. For each
  student there are four possible marks:

        PRESENT          ABSENT          LATE          EXCUSED

  A teacher may also add a short note explaining an unusual mark.

WHY IT MATTERS

  Attendance is one of the strongest indicators of a child's progress. A pattern
  of lateness or absence is often an early signal that a student needs support.
  It is far easier to act on when a teacher can see the whole picture rather
  than relying on memory.

WHAT IT BRINGS TO THE SCHOOL

  -  Teachers save the time previously spent writing and retyping registers
  -  Patterns of absence become visible early, so support can be offered sooner
  -  Students see their own attendance record and percentage
  -  The school gains accurate attendance data for its own reporting

MADE PRACTICAL FOR BUSY TEACHERS

  Two features were included specifically because marking thirty students by
  hand is slow.

  A teacher can tap "ALL PRESENT" and then change only the few students who are
  not there. The previous day's register also loads as a starting point.

  If a register is marked in error, the whole day can be cleared and redone in
  one action. A student who is not selected keeps their existing mark, so
  correcting one pupil never disturbs the rest of the class.



================================================================================
 2.  A HOLIDAY CALENDAR
================================================================================

WHAT IT DOES

  Public holidays and school closures are recorded in one shared calendar, each
  with a date and a short description.

WHY IT MATTERS

  A calendar that says "Independence Day - school closed all day" removes a
  great deal of confusion for families. It also prevents the situation where a
  teacher marks an entire register of absences on a day the school was actually
  shut.

WHAT IT BRINGS TO THE SCHOOL

  -  Parents and staff always know the next closure, and why
  -  The attendance page warns a teacher before they mark a register on a holiday
  -  No more registers filled with absences on a public holiday


================================================================================
 3.  AN ASSIGNMENT HUB WITH IMAGE SUBMISSION
================================================================================

WHAT IT DOES

  Teachers post assignments, with a title, subject, class, due date and
  instructions. They may attach photographs of questions, diagrams or scanned
  sheets.

  Students open the assignment, read the instructions, and upload photographs
  of their own written work as their answer.

WHY IT MATTERS

  This addresses a real limitation of a digital platform. Not every child has
  reliable internet access at home, and a child working in an exercise book
  cannot "submit" it. Being able to photograph the work and upload it removes
  that barrier entirely. The teacher receives the real work, exactly as written,
  in the child's own hand.

WHAT IT BRINGS TO THE SCHOOL

  -  Students who would otherwise be held back by poor connectivity can still
     complete and submit their work
  -  Teachers receive clearer, more complete work than a typed answer alone
  -  Nothing is lost. A student may upload several images, and may re-upload if
     they realise they photographed the wrong page
  -  Teachers record scores against each assignment and give written feedback

TEACHERS CAN BE SELECTIVE WHEN GRADING

  As with exam results, a teacher may enter marks for only the students who
  actually submitted. A blank entry is never treated as a zero, so a student
  who missed one assignment is not unfairly penalised.

================================================================================
 5.  NEWS, ANNOUNCEMENTS AND TESTIMONIALS
================================================================================

WHAT IT DOES

  The school can now publish news, announcements, events and achievements on
  the public website, with photographs. The school can also publish testimonials
  from parents and students.

WHY IT MATTERS

  Previously the front page was a fixed design that could only be changed by
  someone who could edit the website code. That meant school news was rare,
  slow, and out of date. Now the school office can publish an item the same
  day.

WHAT IT BRINGS TO THE SCHOOL

  -  Parents hear about events, results and achievements as they happen
  -  The public page reflects the life of the school rather than a brochure
  -  Testimonials give prospective parents a genuine sense of the school's
     character, written by the community rather than by the school about itself

  Posts can be written in advance and saved as a draft before they go live, so
  nothing appears publicly by accident. Once published, a post can be hidden or
  removed at any time.


================================================================================
 6.  RESULTS ENTERED THE WAY TEACHERS ACTUALLY WORK
================================================================================

WHY IT MATTERS

  Marking is now more forgiving in a way that is easy to overlook but very
  important. A student who was unwell and sat only some of the examinations is
  no longer disadvantaged. Their results are recorded for the subjects they did
  complete.

  Marking is also now checked for errors. A mark that would produce a score
  above the maximum is rejected with a clear explanation, so an accidental
  keypress cannot quietly distort a child's record.

PROMOTION HAS ALSO BEEN MADE SAFER

  Previously a student could be moved up a class on the strength of a single
  subject being entered. A student is now promoted at the end of term only when
  every subject for that term has been recorded.

  Partial entry remains fully supported. It simply no longer triggers a
  promotion on its own.

 8.  A MODERN, EASIER-TO-USE DESIGN
================================================================================

The entire platform has been redesigned. The most useful changes are not
cosmetic.

IT WORKS PROPERLY ON A PHONE

  The menus, forms, tables and buttons now rearrange correctly on small
  screens. This matters more than it sounds. A parent checking results from a
  phone during their lunch break is a realistic scenario, and it previously did
  not work well.

A LIGHT AND DARK THEME

  The platform follows the setting of the phone or computer automatically, and
  can be changed with a single tap. Reading a results table in bright sunlight
  is far easier in light mode. Working at night is far easier in dark mode.

CLEARER FEEDBACK

  When something is saved, the platform confirms it immediately in the corner
  of the screen, rather than pushing the page content downwards with a large
  coloured banner. If something needs attention, it is explained.

FASTER TO USE

  Filters and search now work across students, staff, classes and assignments.
  Forms show exactly what is required, and errors are explained rather than
  merely rejected.

ACCESSIBLE BY DESIGN

  Larger tap targets, clear text contrast, full keyboard support, and a
  skip-to-content option for screen readers.


================================================================================
 9.  SIMPLER STAFF RECORDS
================================================================================

WHAT IT DOES

  Adding a member of staff now requires only three details:

        NAME          PHONE NUMBER          CLASS

  The staff identification number, email address and an opening password are
  generated automatically.

WHY IT MATTERS

  Not every member of staff manages a class. The bursary, kitchen and
  administrative staff do not. Class is therefore optional, and can be
  assigned later whenever it is appropriate.

  The school office can also view every staff login from one page, which means
  a teacher who has forgotten their password can be helped in a moment rather
  than locked out.

================================================================================
 A NOTE ON PRIVACY AND DATA
================================================================================

Student information is among the most sensitive material the school holds. We
have taken steps to ensure that:

  -  No database file, and no backup of one, is stored in the public code
     repository

  -  The repository is configured so that student records and uploaded
     photographs can never be added to it by accident

  -  Passwords are scrambled, so they cannot be read even by someone with access
     to the school's files

We will continue to review how student data is handled as the platform grows.

  every plan across the school, which makes supervision and quality assurance
