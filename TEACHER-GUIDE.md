================================================================================
                     STARLIGHT MODEL SCHOOL
                   TEACHER PLATFORM GUIDE
================================================================================

                   EMPOWERING MINDS, SHAPING FUTURES.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A practical guide to what is new on the school platform, written for teaching
staff. This covers what you can do, how to do it, and a few things worth
knowing so nothing surprises you.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A NOTE ON YOUR PASSWORD

  When you are added to the platform you are issued a starting password, and
  you will be asked to replace it the first time you sign in. You cannot reach
  any other part of the platform until you do.

  Choose something you will remember. If you forget it, the school office can
  reset it for you. The new password is shown on the staff page on the
  administrator's dashboard, so you do not have to keep track of it yourself.


================================================================================
 1.  MARKING ATTENDANCE
================================================================================

WHERE TO FIND IT

  Sidebar, under Teaching, then "ATTENDANCE".

WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE

  Your classes are listed. Choose one to open its register. Each student has a
  row with four buttons:

        PRESENT          ABSENT          LATE          EXCUSED

  The chosen button turns solid, so at a glance you can see who is in. There is
  also a short note field beside each student for anything worth recording.

THE FAST WAY

  Marking thirty students one by one is slow, so two shortcuts are provided.

  -  Tap "ALL PRESENT" and then change only the students who are not there.
     This is how most registers are done and takes a few seconds.

  -  Yesterday's register loads automatically as a starting point, so if nothing
     much has changed you are already most of the way there.

MAKING A CORRECTION

  Tap the student and choose the right mark. Only that student changes. Nobody
  else is affected, and a student you do not touch keeps the mark they already
  had.

  If an entire day was marked in error, use "CLEAR THIS DAY" to remove the whole
  register and start again.

CHANGING THE DATE

  Use the date box at the top. You can go back and correct any previous day, or
  forward if you are marking ahead.

HOLIDAYS

  If the date you have chosen is a public holiday, a warning appears at the top
  of the register naming the holiday. You can still mark, in case it was a
  normal teaching day, but it is there to stop the mistake happening.

WHAT STUDENTS SEE

  Students can see their own record, with a percentage and a breakdown of each
  day's mark. They cannot see anyone else's, and they cannot mark anything.


================================================================================
 2.  POSTING ASSIGNMENTS
================================================================================

WHERE TO FIND IT

  Sidebar, then "ASSIGNMENT HUB". Choose "POST ASSIGNMENT".

WHAT YOU CAN SET

  Title, subject, class, due date and the maximum score, plus written
  instructions.

  SUBJECT AND CLASS ARE TYPED, NOT CHOSEN FROM A LIST

  You can write anything you actually teach, including a class that has not
  been formally created yet. The platform will link it to a real class
  automatically if one matches.

ATTACHING THE QUESTIONS

  If the work is easier to show than to describe, attach photographs. Useful
  for a question written on the board, a diagram, or a scanned worksheet.

  You may select several images at once, and add more later without losing the
  ones already there. Each image can be removed on its own.

MARKING THE WORK

  Open the assignment to see the score table. Each student shows:

  -  How many images they have submitted, with a button to open them
  -  A score box and a feedback box
  -  An ABSENT tick box for a student who did not do the work

================================================================================
 3.  WEEKLY LESSON PLANS
================================================================================

WHERE TO FIND IT

  Sidebar, then "LESSON PLANS".

WHAT YOU CAN RECORD

  Title, subject, class, the week it covers, and four sections:

        LEARNING OBJECTIVES
        CLASS ACTIVITIES
        TEACHING RESOURCES
        HOMEWORK

  You can also attach supporting images, such as a diagram or a worksheet you
  intend to use.

WHO CAN SEE THEM

  YOUR OWN PLANS ARE PRIVATE TO YOU. Only you and the administration can see
  them. A teacher cannot see another teacher's lesson plans.

  Administrators can view every plan in the school, which is what makes
  supervision and curriculum review straightforward.

SEARCHING

  Use the filter at the top to narrow plans by subject. Useful when reviewing
  what has been covered across a class over the weeks.

WHEN A CLASS NEEDS COVER

  Because the plan is written down and attached to the class, a colleague
  covering for you can pick it up directly rather than starting from scratch.


================================================================================
 4.  ENTERING EXAM RESULTS
================================================================================

WHERE TO FIND IT

  Sidebar, under Administration, then "RESULTS". Search for the student and
  choose "ADD RESULT".

PARTIAL ENTRY IS SUPPORTED

  This is the most important thing to know. A student who was unwell and sat
  only some of the subjects is not disadvantaged.

  -  Leave a subject BLANK if there is no mark to enter
  -  A blank subject is simply skipped
  -  Anything already saved for that subject is left exactly as it was
  -  Saving again with a few subjects does not erase the others

  So you can enter results over several sittings, and correct one subject later
  without touching the rest.

================================================================================
 5.  VIEWING YOUR CLASS
================================================================================

  Sidebar, then "DASHBOARD" shows your class, how many students are in it, the
  number of assignments you have posted, your lesson plans, and how many marks
  you have recorded.

  From the class page you can see the full student list. You can only open your
  own class. Attempting to open another teacher's class is refused.


================================================================================
 6.  THE HOLIDAY CALENDAR
================================================================================

  Sidebar, then "HOLIDAYS". Every public holiday and school closure is listed
  with its date and a description.

  The attendance page checks this list and warns you before you mark a register
  on a holiday, so an entire class is not marked absent on a day the school was
  shut.

  Anyone signed in can view the calendar. Only an administrator can add, change
  or remove an entry, so the list stays accurate and consistent.


================================================================================
 7.  CHANGING YOUR PASSWORD
================================================================================

  Sidebar, then "CHANGE PASSWORD".

  You will need your current password, then the new one twice. If you have just
  signed in for the first time, this is where you will be taken automatically.

  Once your password is changed the platform is fully unlocked, and the setting
  is remembered for next time.

  IF YOU FORGET IT

  The school office can reset it. The starting password is worked out from your
  name, so the office can tell you over the phone without needing to send
  anything.


================================================================================
 8.  USING THE PLATFORM ON A PHONE
================================================================================

  Everything works on a phone, including marking a register. The layout
  rearranges for small screens, and the tap targets are large enough to use
  quickly with one hand.

  The menu button is at the top left. Tapping outside the menu, or the close
  button, closes it.

LIGHT AND DARK

  The platform follows your phone or computer setting automatically. Use the
  sun or moon button at the top right to switch it yourself.

  Light mode is easier to read outdoors. Dark mode is easier on the eyes at
  night.


================================================================================
 9.  WHAT YOU DO NOT NEED TO WORRY ABOUT
================================================================================

  -  You do not need to manage any files. Adding a profile picture, an
     assignment image or a lesson plan diagram simply works.

  -  Old files no longer in use have been removed from the platform. If you
     were shown an earlier or temporary version of something, use the current
     one.

  -  Saving a register, a set of results or an assignment is a single action
     and is confirmed as soon as it completes.

  -  If you are interrupted while entering a long list, the marks you have
     already saved are safe. Nothing is lost by starting again.

  -  If a subject or a student is left blank, that is treated as "not recorded
     yet", never as a zero.


CHECKING YOUR MARKS

  Marks are validated on saving.

  -  Each score must be between 0 and 100
  -  A test and exam combined cannot exceed 100
  -  Anything invalid is refused with an explanation, and nothing is saved for
     that subject

  This prevents an accidental keypress from quietly distorting a student's
  record.

PROMOTION

  Promotion happens at the end of Term 3, and only once EVERY subject for that
  term has a result recorded. If a term is not complete, no student is
  promoted and the platform will tell you how many subjects are still
  outstanding.

  If you need to promote an individual student outside the normal process, an
  administrator can do so from the student list.
